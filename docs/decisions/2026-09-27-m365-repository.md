# Decision: Arcadia is the M365 repository

**Status:** accepted
**Decider:** Shane Skwarek
**Date:** 27 September 2026

## Context

Shane confirmed the plan in `docs/m365-repository-pivot.md` (Project store) with "Let's execute the plan," then narrowed the audience: build slices 1–6 for the superadmin role first and refine permissions later.

Where that plan and this file's product direction differed, the plan wins. Governance the plan says does not move still wins: humans decide and sign; inform, don't indict; dormant instruments stay dormant and do not accumulate; credentials stay in gatekeepers; capability × membership; exclusions at ingest; Cloudflare only.

## Decision

Arcadia aggregates the S-FX Microsoft 365 tenant and keeps a few records Microsoft does not store well. The weekly output is a factual run-sheet. She does not author EOS prose.

Locked defaults:

1. **Pivot.** This is the direction.
2. **Org chart.** Overlay. No Entra PATCH. No `User.ReadWrite.All` or `User.ReadUpdate.All`. Reporting lines stay `users.lead_email` until an app-only manager proof call returns a manager. The proof runs only when credentials exist; otherwise it is logged as skipped. A failed call is not a manager.
3. **Objectives.** All group-owned Planner plans, including plans on client Teams. Roster plans are omitted and counted as not listed. The new index is superadmin-only. The project-scoped board the team already uses stays on `view_board`.
4. **Chat and private channels.** Out. No chat binding. No chat-message ingest. No channel message bodies in this build (plan slices 7 and 8).
5. **Continuing Education.** A chronicle of Course or Certification. Not the Certification Ledger. `instrument.certification_ledger` stays off. Those tables are not written.
6. **Shift patterns.** A rolling eight-week horizon of real shift instances, skipping dates that overlap confirmed time off. No availability write. No approve or decline of time off. Delete of a posted shift is a separate named action.
7. **Audience.** Every new read and write in slices 1–6 is the existing superadmin role (`shane@s-fx.com` and `alex@s-fx.com`). Specialist, lead, and founder grants are not implemented. The limit is temporary.

## Consequences

- Directory, the Leadership overlay, Continuing Education, shift patterns, the tenant plan index, the Processes catalog, and the weekly run-sheet are superadmin-only until Shane widens `isRepositoryAudience`.
- Loop URLs are a catalog and a client link. Graph is not asked for Loop page text.
- The run-sheet is stored and not sent to the client.
- Dormant modules stay in the tree. v4 code is not deleted.
